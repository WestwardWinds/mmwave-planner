mmWave Coverage Planner

Instructions

Lengths are in meters or feet and inches (switch at the top of the panel); angles are in degrees. The origin is the front-left floor corner: X runs along the width, Y into the room, Z up.

Move around the 3D view

  • Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan.
  • Jump to a preset camera with 3D, Top, Front or Side.
  • Press Full screen to fill the screen; Esc or Exit full screen brings it back.
  • Click a sensor, test point or box to select it, then drag one of its arrows to move it along X (red), Y (blue) or Z (green). Esc or clicking empty space finishes.

Set up the room

  • Enter the width, depth and height.
  • Choose a flat, single pitch or double pitch (gable) ceiling. For pitched ceilings, set the peak height and which wall is low or which way the ridge runs.

Place and aim sensors

  • Add, rename, select and remove sensors in the Sensors section. Each sensor has its own color, and the settings below it apply to the selected one.
  • Type an X, Y, Z position, or drag it in 3D with Move in 3D.
  • Use the Snapping buttons to mount the sensor and aim it the usual way for that spot: Nearest Wall puts it flat on the closest wall, pointing straight out; Nearest Corner tucks it into the closest corner, pointing 45° into the room, level; Nearest Ceiling sets it flush on the ceiling above, matching the ceiling's slope (straight down on a flat ceiling). Walls and corners keep the sensor's height.
  • Aim at room center points the sensor at the middle of the room.
  • Fine-tune with horizontal tilt (pan), vertical tilt (pitch) and roll.
  • Pick a sensor model (HLK-LD2450, the default; HLK-LD2412; HLK-LD2410) or set the horizontal and vertical field of view and max distance yourself.
  • The LD2412 and LD2410 measure distance in gates: gate 0 is at the sensor, then one gate every set distance. Choose the gate spacing (LD2412: gates 0–13 at 0.75, 0.5 or 0.2 m, reaching 9, 6.5 or 2.6 m; LD2410: gates 0–8 at 0.75 or 0.2 m, reaching 5 or 1.6 m). The rings on the cone mark each gate, and the max distance can't go past the last gate. The closer spacings need newer sensor firmware.

Using the Apollo R PRO-1 ceiling mount? Don't tilt or aim the sensor by hand. The recessed can mount can't tilt, so the sensor always matches the ceiling's pitch. Use Nearest Ceiling and leave the tilt as it sets it.

Test spots in the room

  • Add test points where people will be (a sofa, a desk) and move them by typing or dragging.
  • Each point says whether it is detected and by which sensors, with the distance and angles, or why it is missed: out of range, outside the field of view, or blocked.
  • Aim points the selected sensor straight at that point.

Find dead spots

  • Add obstacles (plain box, sofa, bookshelf, bed, kitchen island, partition wall) and set their position, size and rotation. Obstacles block the radar completely.
  • The coverage slice colors the floor plan at a chosen height: each sensor's color where only it sees, dark blue (off-white in dark mode) where two or more overlap, purple for dead spots behind obstacles, and faint red where no sensor reaches.
  • The chips at the bottom of the view show how many points are detected, how much of the floor is covered, how much is seen by two or more sensors, and how much is hidden.

Adjust the display

  • Show every sensor's cone or only the selected one's, clip cones at the walls and ceiling, show range rings, snap dragging to 5 cm (1 inch), and turn the coverage slice on or off.

Save and share

  • Export JSON saves the setup to a file; Import JSON loads one back.
  • Copy share link copies a link that opens the same setup.
  • Reset to example room (click twice) starts over.
